Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our boyswear collections, sizing, shipping, and policies.

Sizing & Fit

What age ranges and sizes do you offer?

Our entire collection is custom-tailored for boys aged 3 to 14 years. Sizes available are 3–4 Years, 5–6 Years, 7–8 Years, 9–10 Years, 11–12 Years, and 13–14 Years.

How do I pick the right size for my child?

We recommend choosing based on your boy's age and height. If he is between sizes or you prefer a relaxed, streetwear-inspired drape, sizing up one year bracket is recommended. You can also view our size chart modal on any product page.

Products & Fabric

What apparel categories does THE YOUTH LABEL produce?

We create premium boyswear including loopknit script sweatshirts, breathable textured t-shirts, tailored button-down shirts, utility cargo joggers, and matching co-ord sets.

What fabrics and care instructions are recommended?

Our apparel is crafted from 100% natural breathable combed cotton and soft loopknit fleece. Machine wash cold with similar colours on gentle cycle, tumble dry low, and warm iron inside out to preserve typographic script prints.

Shipping & Delivery

How fast will my order arrive?

Orders are dispatched from our warehouse within 24 hours. Metro deliveries typically arrive in 2–3 business days, while other locations across India take 4–5 business days.

Are there any shipping charges?

Standard delivery is 100% FREE on all prepaid and Cash on Delivery orders nationwide.

Can I track my order?

Yes. Once your order is placed, you can track its step-by-step progress (Order Placed → Shipped → Out for Delivery → Delivered) in the My Orders section of your account.

Payment Options

What payment methods do you accept?

We actively support Cash on Delivery (Pay on Delivery). Online gateway payments via UPI, Credit/Debit cards, and Net Banking are in progress for upcoming releases.

Is Cash on Delivery available at my PIN code?

Yes, Pay on Delivery is available across most serviceable PIN codes across India through our logistics network.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your exchange and return policy?

We offer a 7-day hassle-free size exchange policy. If an item does not fit comfortably, you can request an exchange for another size within 7 days of delivery, provided tags remain intact and the garment is unworn.

How do I initiate a size exchange or support inquiry?

Simply message our support team on WhatsApp at +91 8838666712 or email us at support.theyouthlabel@gmail.com with your order number.

Still have questions?

Our team is happy to help you with sizing advice, fabric details, and order questions.
